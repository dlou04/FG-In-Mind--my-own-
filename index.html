Our Mission

FG In Mind supports first-generation college students by highlighting lesser-known opportunities for academic and professional growth.

What You'll Find

Scholarships & Grants

Curated funding opportunities including niche scholarships, state grants, and aid programs often missed by traditional advising channels.

Fellowships

Professional advancement programs like Teach For America, Girls Who Code, and other fellowships designed to expand access and experience.

Job Boards

Job and internship boards tailored to students seeking early career exposure and flexible opportunities while in school.

Long-Term Vision

FG In Mind will continue to grow beyond college campuses, providing high school students with early awareness of resources that reduce stress and increase confidence when preparing for college and life beyond it.
